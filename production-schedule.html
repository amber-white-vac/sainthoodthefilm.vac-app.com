Production Schedule

Shooting between Idaho Falls, ID and Salt Lake City, Utah, underscoring the arid American Landscape and Route 66 inspired territories.

Production Window September 1-10
Primary Base Idaho Falls, ID
Visual Route Pocatello to Salt Lake City
Sept. 1

Road Visuals & Motel Area

Day-to-night drone footage, Johnny and Rebecca riding, motel-area exteriors, riding dialogue, and golden-hour intimacy coverage.

Sept. 2

Basement Scene & Gas Station

Daytime basement punishment scene followed by nighttime gas station coverage with Rebecca and Johnny.

Sept. 3

Motel & Dixie's Diner

Daytime motel scene with featured and background performers, then Dixie's Diner Scene 1 at night with Luthor, waitress, customers, and rowdy men.

Sept. 4

Dixie's Diner Scene 2

Continued diner coverage with Rebecca, Johnny, Luthor, and Raphael.

Sept. 5

Industrial Alleyway, Saloon & Vehicle Work

Rodney voiceover capture, industrial alleyway coverage, roadhouse saloon scene, and nighttime in-vehicle driving scene.

Sept. 6

Crew Off / Pickup Hold

Planned crew rest day with daytime pickups available if needed.

Sept. 7

Fight Choreography & Final Scene Blocking

Blocking and stunt preparation for the final confrontation sequence with Johnny, the four men, muscle guys, and Rodney.

Sept. 8

Pickups, Choreo Refinement & Confrontation Intro

Daytime pickups and fight choreography refinements, moving into nighttime confrontation coverage.

Sept. 9

Confrontation Scene & Luthor Debrief

Nighttime confrontation sequence coverage with Johnny, Rebecca, Luthor, Rodney, four men, muscle guys, and Bound Man #2.

Sept. 10

Luthor Debrief & News Media Scene

Final debrief coverage followed by the night news and media scene with news anchor, news crew extras, police sirens, and Luthor.

Key production needs include drone operations, camera and sound vehicle coverage, stunt coordination, background performers, and HMUA support on motel and final confrontation shoot days. Exact call times and private location details are distributed directly to cast and crew.
