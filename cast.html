Sean Whalen

as "Rodney"

Sean was born in Washington D.C. The youngest of four, he was raised in Silver Spring/Olney Maryland and graduated from Sherwood High School. He moved west and graduated from UCLA four years later. Like most actors, he worked as a waiter while studying and performing at the famed Groundling Theater and Playhouse West. His break came doing commercials and his first film, The People Under the Stairs (1991). Sean has worked in every genre of film and television. (Except a Western. He really wants to do a Western) He is also an acting coach, writer, producer, looper, acting teacher and heads the comedy department at Studio 4: James Franco's Film & Acting School in North Hollywood. He is a father of two beautiful daughters.

Orease Bowers

as "Luthor"

Orease Bowers is an American actor, writer and voice over artist from New York City. A fan of "Classic Hollywood" as a child, he was fascinated by the way the actors, were able to create an emotion in him and make him feel like he was part of the scene. He dreamed of one day being on the screen and doing the same. Though he took a different path and was prolific in sports, his love for acting never wavered. He would act out scenes from his favorite movies with his friends and he was urged to pursue it. He has co-starred in the Emmy nominated and award winning shows Netflix's Orange Is The New Black, Showtime's Billions, NBC's The Blacklist and most recently, the critically acclaimed This Is Us.

Hondo Hendricks

as "Johnny Pierdot"

Hondo Hendricks is a dynamic young actor with a magnetic screen presence and an old-soul charm that captivates audiences. Born and raised in the United States, Hondo speaks fluent American English and brings an authentic, grounded energy to his performances. Known for his charismatic on-screen appearance and natural intensity, Hondo has already begun making waves in the local filmmaking scene. With a unique blend of classic Hollywood appeal and modern charm, Hondo Hendricks is a name to watch.

Amber White

as "Rebecca Dawson"

Amber White is a seasoned performer with credits including series on Amazon Prime, Nat Geo, PBS, TLC, and many others. With a stark visual contrast to traditional romantic leads, she brings a raw and impossibly resilient character integrity to all her roles. Trained in classical theater and cabaret, her performances blend deep emotional literacy with a stage actor's range and talent.
