Orease Bowers

as "Luthor"

Orease Bowers is an American actor, writer and voice over artist from New York City. A fan of "Classic Hollywood" as a child, he was fascinated by the way the actors, were able to create an emotion in him and make him feel like he was part of the scene. He dreamed of one day being on the screen and doing the same. Though he took a different path and was prolific in sports, his love for acting never wavered. He would act out scenes from his favorite movies with his friends and he was urged to pursue it. He has co-starred in the Emmy nominated and award winning shows Netflix's Orange Is The New Black, Showtime's Billions, NBC's The Blacklist and most recently, the critically acclaimed This Is Us.
