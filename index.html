Series Overview

"Sainthood" follows John (Johnny) Pierdot and his former drug-addicted girlfriend Rebecca as they hunt down the truly evil criminals of the world who have escaped justice. Men and women who have no intention of repentance for mass murders, human trafficking, child abuse, etc. Those who are permanently devoid of their humanity. Johnny receives telegrams from an unspecified benefactor tipping him off to each new case, and together they travel across the country on his bike to track down these people and deliver what Rebecca believes is simple vigilante justice.

Little does Rebecca know that Johnny is actually the incarnation of God's Avenging Angel, and has been on earth for more than 1200 years, enacting the "karma" that is desperately deserved.

When Johnny's work is complete, he usually reincarnates with a new body to avoid suspicion, but his love for Rebecca keeps him grounded in his current human form. Now he suspects he might have a chance at experiencing something besides vengeance in his own life. He needs her to stay alive and with memory as much as she needs him to stay sober in her reality.

Rebecca, who goes by "Roach" (never Becky), has survived more than her fair share in life, and is the purest soul Johnny has ever encountered. Themes of "if this didn't happen, who could you have been?", Hope, Revenge, Fantasy vs. Reality, and the Purpose of God and/or Moral Philosophies are all central to the series.

Rebecca's character arc culminates the "Sainthood" series - she becomes the Divine Counterpart to the Angel of Vengeance, given transcendence and the title of Angel of Mercy. Later, this opens the series for time-period focused settings with Johnny and Rebecca carrying out their work in different eras as the same duo that battles for glory against all the odds that were once against them.

"Sainthood" fits into the genre-heavy early-2000s era series that gained large cult followings such as Charmed, Buffy the Vampire Slayer, Supernatural, Vampire Diaries, etc. and is currently in pre-production with anticipated release date of the end of 2026.
