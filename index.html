Logline: "Sainthood" is a genre-heavy, early-2000s inspired cult series that follows Johnny Pierdot and Rebecca Dawson as they ride cross-country hunting demonic forces inhabiting the world's most evil criminals. Little does Rebecca know that Johnny's quest for simple vigilante justice has a much higher calling, and together they must face the consequences of his true identity: the human incarnation of God's Avenging Angel.

Series Overview

"Sainthood" follows John (Johnny) Pierdot and his former drug-addicted girlfriend, Rebecca Dawson, as they hunt the demons that infest truly evil criminals of the world. When Johnny's work is complete, he is meant to reincarnate with a new body and avoid humans tracing his movements. But this time, falling for Rebecca has kept him grounded in his current human form. Now he suspects he might have a chance at experiencing a life with a meaning beyond vengeance-- and he needs her to stay alive as much as she needs him to stay sober.

Here, Johnny is content with fighting low-level offenders, and is reluctant to hunt at any demons that awaken his Divine Angelic forces. However, when he involves himself with Rebecca, a stronger Demon awakens: One that does not just fight for human souls, but one hell-bent on destroying the Angels themselves. Together they travel cross-country on his bike tacking the signs of deeper evil at the will of Luthor, the human incarnation of the Archangel Michael, and God's middle-management.

Rebecca's character arc culminates the "Sainthood" series as she is exposed to the Angelic realms moreso than any other human before her, and her true nature is revealed to be the Divine Counterpart to the Angel of Vengeance-- The Angel of Mercy.

"Sainthood" fits into the genre-heavy early-2000s era series that gained large cult followings such as Charmed, Buffy the Vampire Slayer, Supernatural, and Vampire Diaries. Episode 1 is currently in pre-production with anticipated release date at the end of 2026.
