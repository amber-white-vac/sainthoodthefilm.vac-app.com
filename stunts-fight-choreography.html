Rich Chavez

Stunt Coordinator & Fight Choreographer

AKA "The Freaking Beard Man." Rich Chavez is known for NCIS: Hawai'i (2021), The Chosen (2017), and American Murderer (2022). A professional Stunt Coordinator with exceptional talent and originality, Rich has built a reputation as one of the premier stunt professionals in the American Midwest and beyond. His ability to blend dynamic action, cinematic storytelling, and performer safety makes him an invaluable creative partner for productions of every scale.

Ron Waterman

Fight Choreographer & Stunt Performer

Professional UFC and WWE fighter Ron Waterman is the 2006 Super Heavyweight Champion of the World. With more than fifteen years of professional coaching experience, Ron brings a disciplined, grounded approach to stunt performance and an instinctive understanding of cinematic fight choreography. He also appeared in the very first UFC PlayStation games. Outside of entertainment, Ron serves full-time as a Firefighter/Paramedic with Mountain View Fire Rescue in Longmont, Colorado. A certified EMT, CPR instructor, and member of the National Wildland Team, he combines elite athleticism with real-world emergency response experience. Ron resides in Berthoud, Colorado with his wife Daisy and enjoys spending time with his growing family.

Justin Sloan

Stunt Performer, Actor & Performance Coach

Justin Sloan is a stunt performer, actor, and performance coach based in Utah, known for bringing authentic movement and athleticism to action-driven storytelling. With nearly two decades of experience in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, wrestling, boxing, and strength and conditioning, he combines technical skill with professionalism both on and off set. Justin also hosts the Justin Our Minds podcast, where he shares conversations centered on resilience, mindset, and human performance.
