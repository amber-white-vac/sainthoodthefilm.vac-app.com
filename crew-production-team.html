Production Team

Director

Avaah Blackwell

Avaah Blackwell is a multilingual actress and versatile stunt performer. Classically trained in Europe, Blackwell began her career as a performer after graduating Prague Film School's "Acting for Film" program at the top of her class. One of ten actors globally selected for PFS's innovative program, Avaah is equally captivating performing for stage and screen. Blackwell is the co-founder of a series of industry related events entitled "Behind the Camera" at Soho House Toronto. Avaah utilizes her international training & experience to infuse her work with unique physical, emotional, and mental discipline. Versatile in her character choices, Blackwell is known best for playing strong female leads, as well as interesting supporting characters. Blackwell's performances are described as "magnetic, electric and enchanting" by producers and audiences across Europe ( Cannes Film Festival, The Bear Theatre Company), Asia (Pi Fan) and North America (TIFF, Los Angeles International Short Film Festival, New York City International Film Festival, Calgary International Film Festival, Golden Panda, Studio 35 Festival, et al.). Blackwell's exceptional comedic performance in the Winnipeg Fringe Festival earned her recognition from CBC. Blackwell's love for improvisation and comedy allows her amiable personality to shine both on and off camera. When not working on screen, Avaah can be found supporting her love for film by working for TIFF and Sundance Film Festival. She also spends her time training in acrobatics, martial arts, learning new languages and loves riding horses with her sister Mick in Alberta.

Director of Photography

Steve Stanulis

Steve Stanulis is an actor, producer, and former NYPD officer known for bringing real-world authenticity to film and television. A decorated police officer whose career shifted after an on-duty injury, Stanulis went on to work private security for high-profile clients, an experience that helped shape his transition into the entertainment industry. After studying at HB Studios in New York, Stanulis built a career spanning major studio films including The Interpreter and I Now Pronounce You Chuck & Larry, as well as television appearances on The Sopranos and HBO’s The Deuce. In recent years, Stanulis has focused on producing and starring in independent films and series, establishing himself as a lead actor and content creator. His feature Monica received Independent Spirit Award recognition, while American Fango, Sam, and The Networker were all acquired by Sony platforms within a single year-an uncommon achievement for an independent filmmaker. Stanulis also starred in 5th Borough, a New York crime drama reflecting his street-level roots, and continues to develop projects grounded in real-life experience. His series Great Kills has gained a growing audience and viral traction, further cementing his presence in the crime and dark-comedy space. Beyond film, Stanulis created the Off-Broadway show Stripped, based on his experiences as a Chippendales performer, which was later picked up for a successful run at Planet Hollywood in Las Vegas and received coverage in The New York Times. Drawing from his background in law enforcement and private investigations, Stanulis continues to develop film, television, and digital content centered on crime, power, and high-stakes human behavior-bridging the gap between real-world experience and cinematic storytelling.
